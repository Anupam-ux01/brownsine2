    FAQs

    Do overnight clear aligners really work?

    Most aligners require daytime wear because they aren’t cleared to deliver consistent forces in a shorter, more concentrated schedule. In contrast, GleamFit Overnight Aligners are specifically FDA-cleared for nighttime use. It uses advanced technology that delivers high-quality results with approximately 50% less wear time, making it perfect for those with demanding schedules who prefer to treat their smile while they sleep.

    What's the difference between GleamFit Aligners and Invisalign?

    GleamFit delivers everything you expect from Invisalign, only better. We’ve taken the proven science of alignment and married it with cutting-edge materials and a revolutionary schedule to create a treatment that is effective, comfortable, and, most importantly, compliant. 

    What you get with GleamFit:

    - 50% less wear time: Only 12 hours, overnight.

    - Smarter design: Precision 3D + HyperElastic™ materials for gentler, more effective movement.

    - Enhanced comfort: Say goodbye to hard, scalloped edges.

    - Next-level results: A professionally supervised treatment that is fully integrated into your life.

    What makes The Gleamery different from my dentist?

    The Gleamery is a dental boutique with licensed, expert Hygienists that provide teeth cleanings and whitenings tailored to our clients’ needs. Dr. Tina Nguyen, our Founder & supervising dentist, sets our standard of care, oversees our Hygiene team, and maintains a robust intake process to ensure all smiles are safe in our studios.

    We remove many of the uncomfortable aspects of traditional dental care (drills, judgment, scheduling, cost) with convenience, affordability, and hospitality.

    Our studios are designed with you in mind, and we offer teeth cleanings and whitenings in as little as 30 minutes to accommodate busy schedules. We offer same-day, evening, and weekend appointments; self-care should never be a burden.

    During your treatment, if one of our Hygienists spots something they think should be reviewed by a dentist, we’ll refer you out to one of our local dental partners (whom we trust) for additional care. But your experience at The Gleamery is designed to be an experience in self-care. To us, smile-care = self-care.

    Do you take insurance, HSA or FSA?

    We accept both HSA & FSA.

    We also accept most major insurance providers including Delta, Cigna, Moda, and more. While in the studio, we will provide you with everything you need, including paperwork and a superbill, to submit to your insurance for reimbursement. Insurance typically covers the majority of the cost of your cleaning, but you will be responsible for the cost of your cleaning while in the studio.

    See full insurance page for more information.

    Please note that insurance is only applicable to our Cleanings & can also be applied to Cleaning & Whitening treatments, but the cost of the Whitening cannot be covered by insurance.

    What’s your late/cancellation policy?

    Cancellations or reschedules within 24 hours of your appointment will incur the full cost of your appointment to your card on file.  However, if you reschedule and complete a new appointment within 7 days, the rescheduled appointment cost will be discounted by 50%.

    We have a 5-minute grace period for late appointments - please be sure to fill out your forms before heading in so check-in can be a breeze.

    Is teeth whitening safe?

    In our hands, yes. Our licensed Hygienists follow strict safety protocols, and will customize the whitening treatment to your sensitivity (multiple whitening concentrations available) and preferences. We use a combination of ADA-approved, dentist-developed whitening products known for their safety and effectiveness. We combine these products with a complementary fluoride polish - effectively lifting your shade while strengthening and protecting your enamel.

    What if I have crowns, veneers, or composites?

    All of our cleaning treatments are totally suitable for clients who have had restorative dental work (crowns, veneers, etc.), but we recommend AirFlow cleanings for these in particular. Our teeth whitening is still very safe for usage on restorative material, but will only impact the shade of any natural teeth in your mouth.

    Should I get a cleaning before I get my teeth whitened?

    As our founder Dr. Tina, says, “You wouldn’t wax your car without getting it cleaned first”. The same applies to our teeth. Though our whitening will still get results, plaque and tartar buildup can reduce the effectiveness of your whitening treatment without a clean canvas. We recommend opting for one of our Cleaning & Whitening duos to reveal your best smile in one visit, or booking a standalone cleaning within one week of your whitening appointment.

    How long does the teeth whitening effect last after a session at Gleamery?

    Whitening is a spectrum, and your whitest shade as well as your results will vary based on a lot of factors such as your skin tone, lifestyle (smoking, consumption of staining foods, etc.), and genetics. Most of our results last somewhere between 3 to 6 months; your Hygienist will provide tips after your session on how to best maintain your smile and suggest when would be best to come back in for a touch-up.

    If my teeth are sensitive after whitening, how long will it last?

    While our whitening treatments are sensitivity-minded, if you experience or are prone to sensitivity it should go away in a couple days. Your Hygienist can provide you with tips as well as recommended products to help keep sensitivity at bay.

    What results should I expect from teeth whitening at The Gleamery?

    Our whitening system is specifically designed for immediate results. If it's your first whitening session, we recommend a 60- or 90-minute whitening to see a strong shade lift (3+ shades). If you're a whitening pro and returning for a touch-up. a 30-minute whitening is optimal for a light shade lift to maintain your shade (best if paired with a cleaning). Naturally, whitening results vary for every client based on a wide variety of factors - including age, coffee/tea/red wine consumption, smoking, and overall tooth enamel health. After your treatment, your Hygienist will discuss your whitening goals and craft a treatment plan to achieve your dream smile. No matter what - we guarantee that you will leave our studio happy and excited about your bright smile.

    What happens if I have a cavity or another type of dental issue?

    If one of our Hygienists spots an area that needs attention from a dentist (like a cavity), we’ll refer you out to one of our trusted local dental partners for additional care.

    Does The Gleamery perform SRPs?

    The Gleamery proudly offers Scaling and Root Planing (SRP) for guests with early to moderate periodontal disease. This advanced cleaning treatment is performed in a beautiful, relaxing environment by licensed dental hygienists, with a personalized plan that prioritizes your comfort, health, and long-term smile aesthetics. If you've been told you need a "deep cleaning," or you’re experiencing bleeding gums, inflammation, or bone loss, our SRP service is designed just for you. We combine clinical precision with a high-touch guest experience.
